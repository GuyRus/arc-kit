• Is the purpose for which you intend to use or

  purpose for which you originally collected it?

• If it is a secondary use or disclosure, would the
  individual reasonably expect you to use or disclose
  it for the secondary purpose?

• If it is a secondary use or disclosure, is it related
  to the primary purpose of collection (or if the
  information is sensitive information, is it directly
  related to the primary purpose?)
