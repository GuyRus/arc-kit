# ARC-901-AITS-v1.0 — AU AI Transparency Statement (Demo)

## Why we use AI
We use AI to reduce processing delays for grants applicants and improve consistency in first-pass triage. Human delegates remain accountable for final administrative decisions.

## Classification
- Usage patterns: Decision-making and administrative action; Workplace productivity
- Domains: Service delivery; Corporate and enabling

## Public interaction/significant impact
The public can interact with an AI-assisted guidance channel. AI suggestions are reviewed by staff before final determination notices are issued.

## Monitoring and protection measures
- monthly quality and drift checks
- incident reporting path for staff and public complaints
- human override and escalation in all adverse cases
- re-validation of impact assessment on material scope/usage/operation change

## Compliance overview
The agency applies the Policy for responsible use of AI in government, maintains accountable officials and use case owners, and maintains an in-scope register.

## Legislative/regulatory alignment
This use case operates under Privacy Act obligations (APPs), records management duties, and agency security controls aligned to PSPF/ISM.
