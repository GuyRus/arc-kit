# UK → AU AI Policy Mapping (Government AI Surfaces)

Scope: AI-only policy surfaces for ArcKit rewrite backlog items **AI-003 to AI-006**.

> This mapping is corpus-driven only (no product file rewrites in this pass).

| UK policy / concept | AU counterpart(s) | Handling | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| UK Government **AI Playbook** (10 principles + ethical themes) | DTA **Policy for the responsible use of AI in Government 2.0**; DTA **AI technical standard**; Finance **National framework for the assurance of AI in government** | replace | AU equivalent is distributed across policy + standard + assurance framework rather than a single “playbook” artefact. |
| UK AI Playbook lifecycle governance | DTA **AI technical standard** lifecycle statements (design/data/train/evaluate/integrate/deploy/monitor/decommission) | replace | AU standard is more lifecycle-control detailed than UK principle-only framing. |
| UK **Algorithmic Transparency Recording Standard (ATRS)** | DTA **Standard for AI transparency statements 2.0** + agency AI transparency statements | replace | AU uses AI transparency statement standard rather than ATRS template nomenclature. |
| UK ATRS central collection/public hub model | No authoritative AU whole-of-government central transparency register identified in corpus | no-equivalent | AU requires statements, but a central publication hub equivalent was not identified in collected official sources. |
| UK DPIA / Article 35 style impact assessment | DTA **AI impact assessment tool** + **Guidance for the AI impact assessment tool** + OAIC **Guide to undertaking PIAs** + OAIC **PIA tool** | replace | AU combines AI-specific impact assessment with privacy-specific PIA obligations. |
| UK GDPR lawfulness/fairness/transparency baseline | **Privacy Act 1988** + OAIC **APP Guidelines** + OAIC AI guidance docs | replace | AU privacy baseline uses APP framework, not UK GDPR lawful-basis model. |
| UK Secure by Design (NCSC framing) | ACSC **Guidelines for secure AI system development** + ACSC **Engaging with AI** + ISM + PSPF AI advisory | replace | AU equivalent is cyber.gov.au + PSPF control stack. |
| UK Cyber Essentials (certification-oriented baseline) | ISM / Essential Eight baseline controls | no-equivalent | No direct AU federal equivalent to Cyber Essentials certification requirement identified in corpus. |
| UK NCSC Cyber Assessment Framework (CAF) | ISM + PSPF + Finance assurance reviews | no-equivalent | Functional control overlap exists, but no direct CAF-equivalent framework in collected AU sources. |
| UK Technology Code of Practice | DTA AI policy suite + Finance CPR/assurance + ACSC/PSPF security controls | replace | AU obligations are spread across multiple policies rather than one TCoP artefact. |
| UK spend controls / IPA assurance gates | Finance **Assurance reviews process** + **RMG 106** + National Framework for AI Assurance | replace | Comparable assurance function exists with different governance architecture. |
| UK Digital Marketplace (G-Cloud / DOS) procurement route | AU **CPR 2025** + BuyICT guidance channel + WofG procurement mechanisms | replace | Equivalent procurement outcome exists, but route mechanics and publication formats differ materially. |
| UK commercial AI supplier transparency expectations | OAIC commercial AI guidance + OAIC procurement/privacy checklists + CPR | add | AU corpus adds explicit privacy-procurement checklists from OAIC. |
| UK EqIA-style equality impact treatment in AI lifecycle | Fairness controls in DTA AIA/technical standard; no single federal EqIA mandate identified | no-equivalent | Closest AU equivalents are fairness/contestability sections, not UK equality-law process mirror. |
| UK model cards / datasheets as expected transparency evidence | No mandatory AU whole-of-government model-card requirement identified in corpus | no-equivalent | May still be good practice, but not evidenced as mandatory in collected AU official docs. |
| UK appeal/challenge rights for automated decisions | AGD administrative law context + **ART Act 2024** + **ADJR Act 1977** + DTA accountability controls | replace | AU legal anchors are administrative law review pathways plus AI governance controls. |
| UK public algorithm register pattern | DTA policy references agency internal AI use case accountability/register responsibilities | add | AU emphasis in corpus is agency governance and transparency statements rather than a UK-style central algorithm register. |
| UK MOD **JSP 936 Dependable AI** | Defence-specific AU pathway not identified in public current corpus | no-equivalent | Treated as optional/sector-specific for this AI-only pass. |
| UK guidance references that are UK-jurisdiction specific (GDPR, ATRS naming, NCSC CAF wording) | AU jurisdiction-specific policy/legislative terms | delete | UK-specific legal/policy terminology should be removed in AU rewrite scope unless kept in explicit legacy-UK context. |
