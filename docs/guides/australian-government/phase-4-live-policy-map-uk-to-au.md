# Phase 4 (Live) — UK to AU Policy Mapping

## Scope

Phase 4 covers live/operate governance and evidencing surfaces:

- `servicenow`
- `traceability`
- `story`
- `operationalize`
- `analyze`

Updated across prompt/command pairs, guides, and operational templates.

## Migration Intent

Preserve ArcKit live-operations structure and assurance reporting capability, while replacing UK-default compliance references with AU policy anchors.

## Policy Mapping

| Previous UK-oriented concept | AU replacement used | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| GDS/TCoP operational compliance framing | Digital Service Standard + DX Policy framing | Operational governance structure retained |
| UK cybersecurity references | ASD/ACSC + PSPF/ISM framing | Security controls mapped to AU baseline |
| UK privacy references | Privacy Act 1988 + APP framing | Data and traceability controls aligned to AU obligations |
| GOV.UK service dependency examples | Generic/shared government service dependency examples | Reuse logic retained without UK lock-in |
| UK risk-governance labels in story/analysis artifacts | AU risk-governance wording | Historical/evidence templates now AU-aligned |

## AU Sources Used

| Source | Use in rewrite |
|---|---|
| Digital Experience Policy + Digital Service Standard | Service operations and compliance-check framing |

- `SOURCE_GAP`: exact service-operate KPI and assurance gates are agency-governed and should be tailored per implementation.

## Rewrite Character

- Live-phase command and template structure preserved.
- UK compliance defaults removed from live-path documents.
- AU policy alignment applied with minimal disruption to workflow utility.
