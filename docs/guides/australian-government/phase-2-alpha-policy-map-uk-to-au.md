# Phase 2 (Alpha) — UK to AU Policy Mapping

## Scope

Phase 2 covers Alpha design and market-shaping surfaces:

- `requirements`
- `data-model`
- `research`
- `wardley`

Updated surfaces include prompt/command pairs, guides, and core/plugin templates.

## Migration Intent

Retain ArcKit Alpha design workflow and analytical depth while removing UK-default policy/procurement framing.

## Policy Mapping

| Previous UK-oriented concept | AU replacement used | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| GDPR/DPA-first data model controls | Privacy Act 1988 + APP controls | Data-governance structure preserved; legal anchors switched |
| UK data residency wording | Australian data residency / transfer-control wording | Retains residency controls in AU context |
| UK marketplace routes (Digital Marketplace/G-Cloud/DOS) | BuyICT/panel sourcing + CPR-compliant procurement language | Keeps build/buy/adopt utility and procurement logic |
| UK wardley procurement narratives | AU sourcing and service ecosystem framing | Mapping method preserved, policy anchor replaced |
| UK-oriented guide references | AU digital policy + security references | Keeps guide usability and traceability |

## AU Sources Used

| Source | Use in rewrite |
|---|---|
| Digital Experience Policy + Digital Service Standard | Requirements/governance quality framing |
| Privacy Act 1988 + APP context | Data-model privacy and transfer controls |
| Commonwealth Procurement Rules (CPRs) | Research and sourcing evaluation framing |
| DTA/digital policy references | AU digital standards positioning |
| ASD/ACSC references | Security posture language in data/service evaluation |

## Source Gaps

- `SOURCE_GAP`: exact jurisdictional transfer mechanisms require agency/legal confirmation per dataset and hosting model.
- `SOURCE_GAP`: agency-specific sourcing panel names and thresholds should be tailored in deployment contexts.

## Rewrite Character

- Alpha-phase command structure retained.
- UK policy and procurement defaults removed.
- AU policy framing embedded without deleting analytical content.
