# AU Defence AI Assurance Pathway Guide

`/arckit.jsp-936` produces an AU public-source baseline assurance pathway suitable for Defence-adjacent assurance packs.

## Important

No public Australian Defence-wide equivalent to UK MOD JSP 936 was identified in the corpus used for this rewrite.

Default output therefore:
- uses AU official public sources only
- includes explicit `SOURCE_GAP` markers where internal Defence policy is required

This command is designed to be **generic-useful** (reusable structure and checklists) without implying internal Defence policy requirements that are not publicly evidenced.

## When To Use

Use `/arckit.jsp-936` when a project needs a single, through-life AI assurance pathway document that:
- defines the AI boundary and decision points
- sets an assurance lifecycle (gates, evidence, exit criteria)
- consolidates governance, oversight, and operational monitoring expectations

## Output

- `ARC-<id>-ADEF-v1.0.md`

## Inputs (If Present)

The generator will read available ArcKit artefacts and connect them into a coherent assurance narrative:
- `ARC-*-REQ-*.md` (requirements)
- `ARC-*-DATA-*.md` (data model)
- `ARC-*-RISK-*.md` (risk register)
- `ARC-*-SECD-*.md` (secure by design, if any)
- `ARC-*-PIA-*.md` (privacy impact assessment, if any)
- `ARC-*-DIAG-*.md` (architecture diagrams, if any)
- `ADR-*.md` (decision records, if any)

## What This Now Produces (Generic-Useful Structure)

The generated document keeps a reusable assurance-pack structure while remaining AU-public-source based:

- system and AI boundary definition + AI component inventory
- public-source policy anchors + explicit internal Defence `SOURCE_GAP` register
- through-life assurance lifecycle plan (gates, evidence, exit criteria)
- core assurance domains (governance, human oversight, privacy/data, safety/reliability, fairness, transparency/records, AI security, monitoring/re-validation)
- minimum evidence checklist + traceability to ArcKit artefacts

## How To Treat `SOURCE_GAP`

`SOURCE_GAP` items are not placeholders for “later writing”; they are a deliberate mechanism to:
- record internal Defence dependencies explicitly
- avoid accidentally inventing Defence-only governance, approvals, or doctrine
- enable Defence owners to overlay internal policy and approval routes without rewriting the entire pack

## Public-source anchors

- DTA AI policy v2.0 (national security carveout)
- PSPF Policy Advisory 001-2025
- ACSC secure AI system development guidance
- ISM/PSPF references

## Relationship To Other Phase 14 Outputs

Common complementary artefacts:
- `/arckit.ai-playbook`: broader responsible AI governance assessment + AI impact assessment (where applicable)
- `/arckit.aits`: AI Transparency Statement for public-sector transparency (where applicable)
- `/arckit.secure`: secure-by-design assessment for the system end-to-end (not only AI-specific controls)
