# AU AI Transparency Statement Guide

`/arckit.aits` generates an AU AI transparency statement aligned to the DTA Standard for AI transparency statements 2.0.

## Output

- `ARC-<id>-AITS-v1.0.md`

## Mandatory statement content

- Why the agency uses AI
- AI usage classification (usage patterns + domains)
- Public interaction / significant impact without human review
- Monitoring and protections against negative impacts
- Policy compliance overview
- Compliance with applicable legislation and regulation
- Most recent update date and public contact email

## Publication controls

- Publish on public-facing agency website
- Review at least annually
- Update on significant change or material accuracy change
- Notify DTA when published/updated
