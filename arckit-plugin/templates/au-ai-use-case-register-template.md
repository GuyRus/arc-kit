## Data Quality and Control Rules

- Unique agency identifier per use case.
- AUCO email must be current and monitored.
- Inherent and residual risk ratings must match approved impact assessment.
- High-risk entries must include review dates and governance pathway.
- Material change to scope/usage/operation triggers register update + AIA re-validation.

- [ ] Register export validated for completeness and latest approved values.
- [ ] AO review completed before submission.

- [ ] Submission evidence stored in project records.
